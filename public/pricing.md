# Pricing — Netsol AI

Netsol AI does not use fixed pricing tiers. Every engagement — AI automation, chatbots, AI agents, web development, or system integration — is scoped individually based on complexity, the number of systems involved, and project size.

## How pricing works

1. **Free consultation** (30 minutes, no commitment) — we discuss your specific situation and what you're trying to solve.
2. **Custom quote** — based on that conversation, we provide a scoped quote for the specific work involved.
3. **No fixed retainer required** — pricing is per-project, not a mandatory recurring subscription.

## Typical timelines

- AI automation: 2–4 weeks
- AI chatbot: 2–4 weeks
- AI agents: 2–4 weeks
- System integration: 2–4 weeks
- Web development: 4–8 weeks (varies more by scope — a marketing site is faster than a custom e-commerce store or web application)
